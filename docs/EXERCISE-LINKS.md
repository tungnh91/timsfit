Generic existing machine labels resolve to the specific lying leg-curl and standing calf-raise demonstrations above; exercise identity and existing saved labels are unchanged. The Romanian deadlift link specifically uses dumbbells, not a barbell or stiff-leg substitute.

The app maps stable exercise IDs locally (including old history) and launches ACTION_VIEW in an external browser with a browsable HTTPS URI. Accessible labels identify the exercise and connection requirement. A missing/disabled browser produces a recoverable message. Links are optional: workout generation, logging, persistence, timer and history stay offline; the app adds no INTERNET permission, embedded player, account or backend.
